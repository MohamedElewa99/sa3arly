# Sa3arly (سعرلي)

## What it is
A mobile-first web app (PWA) for Egyptian used-car traders. It prices a used car before the trader buys it.

## Users
Used-car traders in Egypt (dealers, showroom owners, brokers). They use it on their phones, often standing next to the car, outdoors or in a showroom, sometimes at night. Arabic (Egyptian), right-to-left.

## Core jobs
1. Price a car: photos (AI detects trim and exterior condition), make/model/year/trim, odometer, agency maintenance up to X km, paint checklist per panel (factory / repainted / replaced), mechanical checklist with part-level repairs, then a fair price range with confidence and a line-by-line breakdown.
2. Watch the market: today's zero (new) price changes, the used range moving, and new cheap listings (push alerts).
3. Compare cars before buying: zero price, used range, value retention, number of listings.
4. Revisit recent pricings.

## Product truth
- Prices come from comparable listings (Hatla2ee, OpenSooq, Sylndr) of the same make/model family, year ±1, same trim and similar odometer when there are enough, outliers removed twice; zero prices from Hatla2ee.
- Egypt only. All makes and models.
- Accuracy matters more than anything; every number shows where it came from.

## Brand commitments
- Name: سعرلي, written large in Aref Ruqaa calligraphy.
- Night (black) and day modes, user-switchable.
- Tone: Egyptian Arabic, short and direct, trader vocabulary (فابريكا، رش، متغير، زيرو، عفشة).
